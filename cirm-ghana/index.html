About us

Who we are

The Center for Information Resources Management Ghana (CIRM Ghana) is a not-for-profit organization, incorporated in Ghana in March 2022 as a company limited by guarantee under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992).

Records, data and documents are among the most valuable assets any institution holds. When they are well managed, organizations are more accountable, more efficient and better able to serve the public. CIRM Ghana exists to help institutions get there.

We are based at the Department of Information Studies, University of Ghana, Legon.

What we do

Three ways we make a difference

Our work is built on three pillars that reinforce each other: we speak up for good practice, we build people's skills, and we produce the evidence that guides both.

Advocacy

We promote sound information management policies and practices, and raise awareness of why well-kept records matter.

  • Policy engagement
  • Public awareness
  • Standards and best practice

Training

We equip staff in public and private institutions with practical skills to create, organize, protect and use information well.

  • Workshops and short courses
  • Records and archives management
  • Capacity building for institutions

Research

We study how information resources are managed in Ghana and share findings that help institutions and policymakers improve.

  • Applied studies and assessments
  • Publications and reports
  • Evidence for policy
Who we serve

Partners in public and private life

Public institutions

Ministries, departments, agencies, local assemblies, schools and health facilities that need reliable records to deliver services and stay accountable.

Private institutions

Businesses, NGOs and professional bodies that want to manage their information assets efficiently, securely and in line with the law.
