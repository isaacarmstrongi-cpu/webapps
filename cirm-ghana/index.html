About us

Who we are

The Center for Information Resources Management Ghana (CIRM Ghana) is a not-for-profit organization, incorporated in Ghana in March 2022 as a company limited by guarantee under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992).

Records, data and documents are among the most valuable assets any institution holds. When they are well managed, organizations are more accountable, more efficient and better able to serve the public. CIRM Ghana exists to help institutions get there.

We are based at the Department of Information Studies, University of Ghana, Legon.

Create & capture

Records begin when information is created or received: letters, emails, forms, reports and data. Capturing them properly from day one makes everything that follows easier.

How we help: training on file plans, naming conventions and capturing records correctly.

Why it matters

Good records are good governance

How an institution manages its information affects everyone it serves.

Accountability

Reliable records show what decisions were made, by whom and why, and that is the basis of transparency.

Access to information

The Right to Information Act, 2019 (Act 989) gives the public a right to official information. Meeting it depends on well-organized records.

Data protection

The Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) requires personal data to be handled lawfully and kept secure.

Efficiency & memory

Information that is easy to find saves time and money, and preserves institutional knowledge for the future.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is information resources management?

It is the way an organization creates, organizes, stores, protects, shares and eventually archives or disposes of its records and data, so the right information is available to the right people at the right time.

Who can attend your training?

Our training is for staff of public and private institutions, records and information professionals, and students who want practical skills in managing information.

Can you train staff at our own office?

Yes. We can design in-house training around your institution's needs. Send us a training request and we will get back to you.

How can we partner with you on research?

We welcome collaboration with universities, researchers and institutions. Tell us about your idea.

Is CIRM Ghana a registered organization?

Yes. CIRM Ghana is a not-for-profit company limited by guarantee, registered in Ghana on 14 March 2022 (Company No. CG015780322).
